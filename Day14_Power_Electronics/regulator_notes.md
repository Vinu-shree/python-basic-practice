# Voltage Regulator Experiment

## Objective
To study the working of 5V and 3.3V voltage regulators.

## Observation
Output voltage remained constant even when input voltage varied.
Input must be greater than output voltage.
Regulator wastes extra voltage as heat.

## Disadvantages
Power loss in form of heat.
Low efficiency.
Not suitable for high current without heat sink.

## Conclusion
Voltage regulators provide stable DC output but are inefficient for large voltage differences.
