# Half Wave Rectifier

## Aim
To convert AC voltage into pulsating DC using a single diode.

## Components Required
 1 Diode
 1 Resistor (Load)
 AC Source (Function Generator)
 Multimeter / Oscilloscope
 Breadboard & Connecting wires

## Circuit Description
A half wave rectifier uses a single diode connected in series with the load resistor.

• During positive half cycle:
  The diode becomes forward biased and conducts.
  Output voltage appears across the load.

• During negative half cycle:
  The diode becomes reverse biased and blocks current.
  Output voltage becomes zero.

So only one half of the AC signal passes.

## Working Principle

Input: AC waveform (positive and negative)

Output: Only positive half cycles (pulsating DC)

The diode acts like a switch:
 ON during positive cycle
 OFF during negative cycle

## Waveform

Input:  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  (AC wave)

Output:  ∩    ∩    ∩    (Only positive half)

## Advantages
Simple circuit
Low cost
Easy to implement

## Disadvantages
 High ripple
 Low efficiency
 Poor DC output quality

## Applications
Low power DC supplies
Signal demodulation
Basic rectifier circuits

## Conclusion
Half wave rectifier converts AC to pulsating DC using one diode.
Only one half cycle of AC is utilized, making it less efficient compared to full wave rectifier.
