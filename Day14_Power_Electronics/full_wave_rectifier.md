# Full Wave Rectifier Experiment

## Objective
To convert AC input into DC output using a bridge rectifier.

## Components Used
4 Diodes
1 Resistor (Load)
1 Function Generator
2 Oscilloscope
1 Bread Board
2 Multimeters

## Working Principle
A bridge rectifier uses four diodes to allow both halves of the AC waveform
to contribute to the output, resulting in full wave rectification.

## Observation
Output waveform became only positive.
Frequency of output was double the input frequency.
Voltage drop of approximately 1.4V due to two conducting diodes.

## With Capacitor
Adding a capacitor across the load reduced ripple
and produced smoother DC output.

## Conclusion
Full wave rectifier is more efficient than half wave rectifier
and produces less ripple.
